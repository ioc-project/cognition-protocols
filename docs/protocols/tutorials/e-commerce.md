# E-Commerce

<p class="eyebrow">Specialist systems with incompatible objectives</p>

An e-commerce promotion decided across specialist systems whose objectives genuinely
conflict — the kind of decision where no opening proposal satisfies everyone.

This use case matters disproportionately in the benchmark. It is the one where the
repair loop actually runs: only 20% of runs settle in round 1, against 85–100% on the
other three decisions. The protocol, the floor and the engine are identical across
all of them; what differs is the problem.
